Add first-person camera with free movement; finish cat shading and AI polish

Camera / view
- Add CameraViewMode (Isometric | FirstPerson). V / gamepad Y toggles.
- FirstPerson: 60-degree perspective camera at the player's head (eye
  height 2.2, PersonMesh's head center) with mouse-look yaw/pitch
  (LookSensitivity rad/unit, pitch clamped to +/-85). Yaw is seeded from
  the player's facing on entry; iso pan/zoom/tilt are left intact so
  toggling back restores the exact view.
- Camera.MapMoveToWorld turns WASD into a normalized camera-relative XZ
  heading (W heads where you look, S backs up, A/D strafe).
- Fix the tilt=0 up-vector: always use world Up plus a tiny +Z nudge so
  the view direction is never exactly vertical, removing the one-frame
  roll at the 0/1-degree boundary.

Player movement
- Player.Update(gameTime, map, moveDir, freeMove) now has two modes:
  grid-locked cell-to-cell (isometric) and free world-space (first-person).
- Free mode moves continuously at FreeMoveSpeed with a circle-vs-cell
  collision test (FreeRadius) and per-axis wall sliding. Player.Cell is
  kept synced to the nearest walkable tile so the grid-based critter AI
  and catch check keep working; toggling back to grid snaps the player to
  that cell center so the grid state stays coherent.

Input
- InputState: add Look (first-person look delta, right/up positive) and
  ToggleCamera (rising edge).
- Add IMouseLookSource capability. KeyboardMouseInput captures and
  re-centers the pointer, reporting a relative look delta (skipping the
  capture-start frame so entering FPS does not snap the view);
  InputSystem forwards the request. Game1 hides the cursor and skips
  drawing the player's own body in first-person.
- GamePadInput: Y toggles the view; the right stick also drives look.
- Fix mouse yaw direction (mouse-right now turns right).

Cat rendering
- ObjLoader emits VertexPositionNormalTextureTangent, computes tangents
  from UVs, and bakes the grayscale bump map into a tangent-space normal
  map. Diffuse/normal textures get complete generated mip chains (an
  incomplete chain samples black under a mipmapping sampler).
- Content.mgcb builds Content/Effects/CatNormalMap.fx (SM3, key/fill/back
  lighting matching BasicEffect.EnableDefaultLighting). All effect
  uniforms are pushed from C# because HLSL uniform initializers are not
  reliably applied and zeroed light colors render the cat black.
- CatWalkAnimator procedurally trots the cat's four legs.

Critter AI
- SmartAnimalAI quirk moves: sidestep perpendicular to the player or
  double back to the previous cell (tracked in _lastCell).
